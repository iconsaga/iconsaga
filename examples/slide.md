# Example: icons for a slide

## Yourself, on iconsaga.com

1. Search for what you need in your own words, like "three steps", "handshake" or "growth".
2. Choose a style and your colors.
3. Copy the icon into PowerPoint, or download it for Google Slides or Keynote.

Step by step for each app: [iconsaga.com/powerpoint](https://iconsaga.com/powerpoint)

## With your AI

If your AI makes the slides for you, for example Claude Code or Codex building a PowerPoint file, ask:

> Make a slide about our three-step onboarding: sign up, set up, go live. One icon for each step, in our brand colors, navy and amber.

Your AI finds an icon for each step, gets each one in your two colors, and uses the same style on every slide.
